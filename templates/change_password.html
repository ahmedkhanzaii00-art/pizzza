Change your password securely.

For your protection, your identity is verified again before a password change is accepted. Authentication stays with Firebase while account access remains controlled by the trusted Flask session.

    A fresh Firebase credential is required before the backend accepts this security-sensitive change.

  • Credentials stay out of Firestore

    Current and new passwords are used only for the active authentication operation and are never saved as profile data.

  • Sign in again after success

    The current backend revokes Firebase refresh tokens and clears the Flask session after a confirmed password change.
